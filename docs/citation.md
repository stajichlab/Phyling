## Integrated Tools & Method References

Depending on the modules and options enabled during your analysis, please acknowledge the corresponding tools:

### Profile HMM & Marker Database
- **BUSCO v5**: Simão FA, et al. (2015). BUSCO: assessing genome assembly and annotation completeness with single-copy orthologs. *Bioinformatics*, 31(19), 3210-3212.
- **PyHMMER**: Larralde M. (2023). PyHMMER: a Python library binding to HMMER C code. *Bioinformatics*, 39(5), btad217.
- **HMMER3**: Eddy SR. (2011). Accelerated Profile HMM Searches. *PLoS Comput Biol*, 7(10), e1002195.

### Sequence Handling & Alignment
- **Biopython**: Cock PJ, et al. (2009). Biopython: freely available Python tools for computational molecular biology and genomics. *Bioinformatics*, 25(11), 1422-1423.
- **pyfaidx**: Shirley L, et al. (2015). Efficient random-access FASTA indexing. *Bioinformatics*.
- **Muscle v5**: Edgar RC. (2022). MUSCLE v5 enables high-throughput MSA estimation with high accuracy. *Nature Communications*, 13, 6268.

### Alignment Trimming & Informativeness Filtering
- **ClipKIT**: Steenwyk JL, et al. (2020). ClipKIT: a novel alignment trimming tool designed for accurate phylogenomic inference. *PLoS Biology*, 18(12), e3001007.
- **PhyKIT**: Steenwyk JL, et al. (2021). PhyKIT: a toolkit for the biostatistics and visualization of phylogenomic data. *Bioinformatics*, 37(16), 2325-2327.

### Tree Inference Engines & Consensus Algorithms
- **FastTree**: Price MN, et al. (2010). FastTree 2 – Approximately Maximum-Likelihood Trees for Large Alignments. *PLoS ONE*, 5(3), e9490.
- **IQ-TREE**: Minh BQ, et al. (2020). IQ-TREE 2: New Models and Efficient Methods for Phylogenomic Inference in the Genomic Era. *Mol Biol Evol*, 37(5), 1530-1534.
- **RAxML-NG**: Kozlov AM, et al. (2019). RAxML-NG: a fast, scalable and user-friendly tool for maximum likelihood phylogenetic inference. *Bioinformatics*, 35(21), 4453-4455.
- **ASTER / ASTRAL**: Zhang C, et al. (2022). ASTER: Accurate Species Tree Estimation from Readily Available Gene Trees. *Systematic Biology*.
